Proposed RoboRAG workflow. Training episodes are segmented and checked to form a versioned bank $\mathcal{B}$ of experience graphs $G$. A new goal, current observation and backend capabilities produce a draft plan $P$. Retrieved graphs or subgraphs supply phase structure, conditions and recovery evidence; alignment and validation produce an adapted plan. A supervisor follows approved phases through a local executor, while the planner handles structural revisions. Actual receipts and observations extend the execution graph $X$. The three graphs share a schema, but history, intent and observed execution retain distinct status. The training bank stays fixed during held-out evaluation; the dashed return path is used only by a separately evaluated curation process. This is a method diagram, not an experimental result.
