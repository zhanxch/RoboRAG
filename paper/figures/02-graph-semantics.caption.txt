An interaction graph records phase instances and the evidence connecting them. In this illustrative recorded episode, a grasp attempt fails; reobservation and a second attempt precede placement. Each box is a phase instance, not an object. Solid arrows show the observed temporal order. The dashed recovery link connects the failed attempt to reobservation; the second grasp uses the newly grounded target. The dotted dependency states that placement requires verified holding evidence from the second grasp. Alternative success and failure guards are transition choices (OR), whereas prerequisites must all hold (AND). The node record separates the requested effect from its observed outcome. A plan may contain bounded retry logic; recorded attempts have distinct IDs and times. Times and evidence IDs in this example illustrate the schema and are not measured experimental data.
