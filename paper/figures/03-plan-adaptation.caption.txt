Illustrative retrieval and adaptation for a new task: place a cup in a closed drawer. Two training subgraphs provide object transfer and drawer opening; the target composition is held out from the experience bank. Object roles are rebound to current observations, the open-drawer phase is inserted as a prerequisite, and current geometry supplies the action parameters. The adapted plan also incorporates an authorized recovery branch from historical failure evidence. The supervisor checks predicates and follows the approved branch; structural changes go to the planner. A failed grasp appends a failed event to $X$ and creates a distinct retry instance, rather than relabeling the failed action as successful. The example explains graph composition and revision; it makes no claim that this task has been implemented or solved.
