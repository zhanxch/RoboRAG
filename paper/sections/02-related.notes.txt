Novelty and integration cautions:
1. No first/only claim has been substantiated. The defensible candidate is the specific shared evidence-grounded phase schema and the experimentally attributable value of retaining its relations across stages.
2. A list of three separate established mechanisms does not itself establish novelty. Method/experiments need a causal test of the shared schema, with same-evidence linearization, retrieval/feedback ablations, and matched hierarchy/controller comparisons.
3. The Research position comparison sentence denotes comparable variants with matched interface access, not an unsupported promise to reproduce all published systems exactly; root may phrase the evaluation section accordingly.
4. Keep scenes (objects and relations) distinct from phase graphs (events and transitions); shared object roles do not make the two graph types interchangeable.
5. Main frozen-bank evaluation and chronological accumulation must remain separate, with bank versions explicit.
6. Primary-source summaries are short (each well below 200 words in the chapter). If root adds a comparison table, count the same-source text across prose, table, notes and bibliography together; avoid expanding any one source excessively.
