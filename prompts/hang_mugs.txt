Control the dual ARX X5 robot for hang_mugs. There are three mugs and one mug rack. The robot needs to pick up each mug and hang all of them on the rack.

Completion requirement: All three mugs are hung on the rack and the robot returns to origin.

Use only current RGB, camera calibration and measured robot state. Historical demonstrations are examples. Adapt their object count, positions, dimensions and order to the current scene.
