Control the dual ARX X5 robot for make_toast. There is a basket containing multiple slices of bread and a toaster. The robot needs to pick up two slices one by one, place them into the toaster, and then press the lever down to start toasting.

Completion requirement: Both toaster slots are filled, exactly two bread slices remain on the shelf, the toaster control is pressed down, and the robot returns to origin.

Use only current RGB, camera calibration and measured robot state. Historical demonstrations are examples. Adapt their object count, positions, dimensions and order to the current scene.
