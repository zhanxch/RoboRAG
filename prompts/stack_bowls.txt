Control the dual ARX X5 robot for stack_bowls. There are three bowls. The robot needs to stack all the bowls together.

Completion requirement: All three bowls are stacked together, all bowls are upright, the bottom bowl is strictly and stably placed on the table, and the robot returns to origin.

Use only current RGB, camera calibration and measured robot state. Historical demonstrations are examples. Adapt their object count, positions, dimensions and order to the current scene.
