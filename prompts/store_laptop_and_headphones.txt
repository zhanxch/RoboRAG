Control the dual ARX X5 robot for store_laptop_and_headphones. There is an open laptop on a laptop stand, a vertical laptop stand, a pair of headphones, and a headphone stand. The laptop opening angle is random but greater than 30 degrees. The robot needs to first hang the headphones on the headphone stand, then close the laptop, pick it up from the stand, and insert it into the vertical laptop stand.

Completion requirement: Both the headphones and laptop conditions are satisfied and the robot returns to origin.

Use only current RGB, camera calibration and measured robot state. Historical demonstrations are examples. Adapt their object count, positions, dimensions and order to the current scene.
